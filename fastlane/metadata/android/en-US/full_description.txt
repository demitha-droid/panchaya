Panchaya (පංචාය) is a modern, free, and open-source Android application for streaming and downloading authentic Buddhist Dhamma Sermons.

Features:
- Stream high-quality audio sermons from Jethavanarama Buddhist Monastery and Pahura Dhamma Sermons.
- Sacred Obsidian & Liquid Amber dark theme optimized for OLED battery efficiency and nightly listening.
- Live audio frequency equalizer waveforms synced with playback.
- Offline MP3 download manager for listening in retreat or without internet connectivity.
- Background playback and full MediaSession lockscreen notifications.
- Monastery news reader directly from jethavanarama.org.
- Dhamma shorts video player.
- 100% Free, Open Source, and Ad-Free.
